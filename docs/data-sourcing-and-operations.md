# Data sourcing and operations

**Status:** Proposed operating policy

## Source order

Prefer sources in this order, subject to source terms and availability:

1. Employer's official website, careers page, or direct job listing.
2. Official public filings or employer-published reports for company facts.
3. Reputable sources that clearly attribute their information.
4. Other sources only when their reliability and permitted use have been reviewed.

Search snippets and unverified third-party summaries should not be the sole support for material claims.

## Source record requirements

For each important or time-sensitive fact, retain the source URL, publisher, date checked, field supported, and any use constraints. Show a concise source link to users when practical. Keep the source attached to the specific fact; a generic company homepage may not support employee count or valuation.

## Verification and freshness

Suggested initial operating targets, to calibrate during the pilot:

| Data | Suggested check interval | Stale handling |
|---|---:|---|
| Job status and apply URL | Every 7 days | Mark stale after missed/failed checks; hide or label after 30 days without successful verification. |
| Company website and operating location | Every 90 days | Keep visible with last-checked date; review after a correction or broken link. |
| Employee range | Every 180 days or when a credible update appears | Preserve source date and label as approximate. |
| Public valuation | Only when presenting a dated figure | Display as-of date prominently; do not imply a live value unless refreshed accordingly. |

These are operational starting points, not guarantees. Adjust according to source access, user expectations, and observed posting lifetimes.

## Job lifecycle

1. **Discovered:** A candidate listing is recorded with source URL and first-seen time.
2. **Checked:** Confirm employer, title, location, and application route from the original source where possible.
3. **Published:** Listing is eligible to appear as active with checked date.
4. **Rechecked:** Confirm it remains available and the destination works.
5. **Stale/closed:** Mark stale when status cannot be confirmed; mark closed when source confirms closure or the application destination indicates it is no longer open.
6. **Archived:** Remove from active discovery while retaining only appropriate operational history.

Do not infer that a role is open solely because an old search result remains indexed.

## Company fact lifecycle

1. Create a draft from an identified source.
2. Confirm the employer identity and Perth relevance.
3. Add only fields supported by sources; leave unknown fields empty.
4. Publish with a checked date for changing facts.
5. Recheck on schedule or when users report a problem.
6. Correct, supersede, or archive the fact while retaining source provenance.

## Corrections and takedowns

- Provide a visible route for users and companies to report errors.
- Acknowledge and triage reports; prioritize broken apply links and materially wrong claims.
- Verify corrections against a source before changing factual data where possible.
- Record the report, decision, and update date without exposing reporter details publicly.
- Provide a way to remove or hide content when appropriate, with a documented review path.

## Automation policy

Automation may assist with link checks, change detection, and reminders. It should not silently convert uncertain observations into confirmed facts. Before automated collection or republication, review the source's terms, technical restrictions, data rights, and operational cost. Keep a human review path for ambiguous matches, valuation claims, and user corrections.

## Editorial checklist

- Is this the correct company and is its Perth connection clear?
- Does each material claim have an appropriate source?
- Is employee size shown as a range when precision is unsupported?
- Is company value omitted unless a credible, dated public figure exists?
- Is the job listing original, current, and linked to a working application route?
- Are checked and posted dates clearly distinguished?
- Is any personal or sensitive information unnecessary for the directory?
