# Risk and governance

**Status:** Initial planning register; review before public launch

| Risk | Impact | Initial response |
|---|---|---|
| Stale or closed job posts appear active | Users waste time and lose trust | Show checked dates, recheck regularly, provide reporting, and mark uncertain listings stale. |
| Wrong employer identity or location | Misleads users and companies | Confirm identity and Perth relevance from reliable sources; use review for ambiguous matches. |
| Unsupported size or valuation claims | Reputational and decision-making harm | Use sourced employee ranges; omit private valuations; date and define any public value figure. |
| Incomplete coverage is mistaken for completeness | Users infer businesses are absent or excluded | State selection and coverage limits in the interface; avoid completeness claims. |
| Source terms or data rights restrict reuse | Content may need to be removed or collection stopped | Review each source's terms and permitted use before collection and republication; seek appropriate advice where needed. |
| Automated collection breaks or misclassifies jobs | Incorrect records at scale | Start with manual review; use automation as a signal and log failures. |
| Company or user correction is ignored | Loss of trust and unresolved inaccurate content | Publish a clear correction route, assign an owner, and track response and resolution. |
| Sensitive personal information is collected unnecessarily | Privacy and security exposure | Focus on organizations and job postings; minimize personal data, analytics, and retention. |
| External link or malicious URL risk | User safety or site integrity | Validate destination URLs, clearly label external navigation, and avoid embedding untrusted content. |
| Editorial access or secrets are compromised | Unauthorized changes or service disruption | Restrict admin access, protect secrets, maintain backups, and review access periodically. |

## Governance roles to assign

Before public launch, identify an owner for:

- Data sourcing and source-use review.
- Editorial verification and corrections.
- Application security, backups, and incident response.
- User feedback and support.
- Product decisions and region coverage claims.

One person may hold several roles in a small pilot, but each responsibility should have a named owner.

## Launch readiness checks

- Coverage statements match the actual dataset.
- Every active job has a checked source and working application route.
- Company size and valuation displays communicate uncertainty and dates.
- A correction and removal path is monitored.
- Editorial access, backups, and recovery steps are in place.
- Source use, privacy practices, analytics, and relevant obligations have been reviewed for the actual operating model.
- Users can identify external links and understand where applications take them.

This document is a product planning aid, not legal advice. Source-specific and launch-specific obligations depend on the implementation and should be reviewed with appropriate expertise.
